# Al Qairawan roads: questions for Parsons (draft, 4 Nov 2026)

Raised while writing the 250 roads of LDIP-RCRC-SAP-01-AQ-ROD-DWG-10200-001 (layout) and
ROD-DWG-40000-001 (profiles) into InfraWorks, checked against the grading surface
LDIP-RCRC-LAI-LDIP-01-CIV-DWG-00002-01 ("Al_Qairawan_Mass Grading"). Every value below was read from
these documents; where AICC had to choose, the choice is stated and awaits confirmation.

## Levels

1. **R.1.G.24 / R.1.G.23.** R.1.G.24 starts on loop R.1.G.23 at PGL 679.807 (ROD-40000 profile, as
   printed). R.1.G.23's own profile gives 680.342 at that point: a 0.535 m step at the junction.
   R.1.G.24's other end meets R.1.F.1 at the same level (1.7 cm). Which level is intended?
2. **R.3.G.1.** Over its first ~100 m the profile (PGL ~689) stands up to 5.9 m above the grading
   surface, which falls to ~683.6 there; elsewhere roads sit 0.5-0.8 m above it. Is the profile or
   the grading surface current?
3. **Profiles shorter than their centreline.** The profile stops before the end of the drawn
   centreline; AICC ends each road where its profile ends:
   R.0.D.5 (plan 0+416.889, profile 0+375.000), R.3.G.11 (2.000 m), R.14.G.7 (1.560 m),
   R.13.G.3 (0.600 m), R.14.F.3 (0.328 m), R.12.E.1 (0.095 m), R.10.G.4 (0.058 m).
   Please give levels for the remaining lengths, or confirm the roads end there.
4. **PGL labels off the profile line.** R.14.G.14 and R.3.G.8 each have one PGL row label 0.55 m off
   the drawn FG line (all other labels agree within 1 mm). Which is right?

## Sections

5. **Median kerb (B1, C1, C2).** ROD-30000-001 shows upstand kerbs (150 mm) on both median edges but
   no kerb width; AICC used 0.30 m, as the road kerb. Please confirm.
6. **Roundabouts RA-1 to RA-7.** From the drawn edges: island radius 12.0 m, outer edge 20.2 m,
   circulatory width 8.2 m; kerbs 0.30 m both edges. Crossfall of the circulatory carriageway is
   not given (modelled 0 %; the grading surface is a tilted plane across the rings). Please give the
   roundabout section and crossfall.
7. **R.9.G.6 and R.9.G.7.** The right-of-way is 15 m (A1) at their ends, but on R.9.G.6 the drawn
   centreline is off the middle of the carriageway by up to ~1.8 m, and R.9.G.7's carriageway widens
   on one side to ~9.8 m (ROW 17.4 m) over its middle (a lay-by?). AICC modelled both as A1 on the
   drawn centreline. Please confirm the sections.
8. **One-way crossfall of local roads (A1-A3).** The typical section shows a single fall to a low
   point at one kerb but not which side; AICC read the side per road from the grading surface.
   On these the surface changes side or is unclear along the road; please confirm the side:
   - mixed, modelled falling right: R.6.E.1 R.6.G.1 R.6.G.3 R.6.G.6 R.6.G.9 R.6.G.10 R.6.G.11
     R.6.G.12 R.6.G.24 R.6.G.25 R.3.F.1 R.3.G.1 R.14.G.7 R.14.G.15 R.13.G.14 R.13.G.6
   - mixed, modelled falling left: R.6.F.3 R.13.G.15 R.13.G.5
   - unclear, modelled falling right (the typical section as drawn): R.6.G.14 R.3.G.2 R.4.G.13
     R.11.G.4, and R.6.G.22 (crowned on the surface)
